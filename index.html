Introduction

The Softball Mechanics app uses AI to analyze softball mechanics, including windmill pitching, hitting and catching. We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our App, including children. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Player name — used to personalize analysis reports.
  • Your date of birth (the account holder's) — required at setup to verify your age and decide whether a parent or guardian must own the account. The date itself stays on your device; only the age calculated from it is stored with your account.
  • The player's date of birth — optional, when you are not the player. Used only to set the player's division and keep the analysis age-appropriate. It is stored on your device with that player, not on your account.
  • Email address — optional. Creates a cloud account for cross-device sync, delivers PDF reports, and lets us answer support requests.
  • Marketing preference — whether you ticked the optional box to receive product-update emails. It defaults to no.
  • Recruiting Profile — only if you choose to create one: grad year, positions, high school, travel team, state, height/weight, bats/throws, GPA and SAT/ACT if you enter them, measurables such as running times and exit velocity, and your mechanics scores.
  • Team data — if a coach creates a team: the team name, roster names and the team's scores.
  • Campaign code — if you type one in at setup. It tells us which outreach works; it unlocks nothing.

1.2 Video frames

You choose a video on your device and the App extracts still frames at key moments of the motion. Only those still images — not the video file — are sent to our server for AI analysis, together with the player's name and the details the analysis needs (such as age). The frames are discarded as soon as the analysis is generated. We never receive or store the original video. Player photos you add to a card stay on your device and are never uploaded.

1.3 Account and analysis history

Your profile and session history are always stored on your device. If you create an account with an email, they are also stored in our cloud database so you can use them on another device. Without an email, your data stays on your device only.

On iPhone and iPad your sign-in token is kept in the system keychain, which Apple does not clear when an app is deleted. This is deliberate: reinstalling returns you to your own account instead of a blank one. Deleting the App therefore does not delete your cloud data — use Delete Account in the App, or email us.

1.4 Subscriptions

If you subscribe, Apple or Google processes the payment and RevenueCat tells us whether your subscription is active. We never receive your payment details.

1.5 Error reports

If the App hits a technical error, it sends a diagnostic report containing the error, the screen or feature it happened on, your device model and manufacturer, operating system, app version and build, and your account identifier. Reports do not include your name, your email address, or any player profile information. They are used only to fix technical problems and are never sold or used for marketing.

1.6 We do NOT collect

  • GPS location or precise location data
  • Contacts or address book entries
  • Browsing history or web activity
  • Biometric data or health records
  • Payment card details
  • Advertising identifiers — the App shows no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Name and age generate personalized, age-appropriate mechanics reports.
  • Email creates your optional cloud account, delivers your PDF reports, and is used for app support.
  • We send product-update emails only if you ticked the optional box at setup. You can opt out at any time by emailing us. We never sell your email address and never use it for third-party advertising.
  • Video frames are sent to our server for AI analysis and are not retained afterward.
  • With an account, your profile and session history sync so they are available across your devices.
  • Error reports are used to diagnose and fix technical problems.

We will never sell, rent, or license your personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Softball Mechanics is designed for coaches, parents, and players. Setup requires your date of birth — the account holder's — and it cannot be skipped. We use it to determine when a parent or guardian must own the account. The player's date of birth is optional and is used only to keep coaching age-appropriate; if we do not know a player's age we apply the stricter treatment, not the looser one.

If the player is under 13, a parent or legal guardian must set up and own the account: they confirm they are the parent or guardian and provide their own email address, which becomes the account's owner and must be verified. We then email the guardian a link to confirm consent; the account stays locked and cannot be used until they click it, and we record the date and time consent was given. That email also lets the guardian withdraw consent at any time.

For any player under 13, recruiter and public recruiting profiles are turned off and cannot be switched on. Their profile can be shared with their own team's coaches only, and it is never published to recruiters, to search, or to a public link.

A parent or guardian may review or delete their child's data at any time by emailing mechanicssupport@gmail.com from the guardian address on the account. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we delete it promptly.

6. Data Security

  • All data transmitted between the App and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Our database enforces row-level security: your rows are readable only by your account.
  • API keys and secrets are stored as server-side environment variables and are never embedded in the App.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access — email us for a copy of your data.
  • Deletion — use Delete Account at the bottom of the App's settings, or email us, and we will delete your account and its cloud data. Uninstalling removes the local copy but not the cloud copy.
  • Correction — edit your profile in the App.
  • Withdraw marketing consent — email us; it takes effect immediately.
  • Recruiting visibility — change it or delete the profile in the App at any time.
  • California residents (CCPA): you may know what we collect, request deletion, and opt out of sale — we do not sell personal information — without being discriminated against for exercising these rights.
  • EEA / UK residents (GDPR): you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability, and may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Our legal bases are consent (email, marketing, recruiting publication), contract (delivering the analysis you asked for), and legitimate interest (diagnostics, security, and knowing which outreach brought you here). Where the user is a child, consent is given by the holder of parental responsibility.

To exercise any of these, contact mechanicssupport@gmail.com.
