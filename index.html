Introduction

The Softball Mechanics app uses AI to analyze softball mechanics, including windmill pitching and hitting. We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our App, including children. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Voluntary Information:

  • Name (required) — used to personalize analysis reports.
  • Email address (optional) — used only if you choose to receive results by email.

Video Frames:

The app captures still frames from videos stored on your device. We do not receive, store, or retain the original video file. Only compressed JPEG frames are sent to our server for AI analysis and are discarded immediately afterward.

Error Reports:

If the app encounters a technical error, an automatic diagnostic report may be sent. These reports include device operating system, app version, and the name and email address you provided (if any).

We do NOT collect:

  • GPS location or precise location data
  • Contacts or address book entries
  • Browsing history or web activity
  • Biometric data or health records
  • Financial information or payment details

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • Personalizing your analysis reports with your name
  • Delivering analysis results to your email address (only if provided)
  • Generating AI-powered mechanics analysis via Anthropic's API
  • Diagnosing and fixing technical errors in the app

We will never send you marketing emails or promotional messages. We will never sell, rent, or license your data to third parties.

3. Information Sharing

We share data only with the following service providers, and only as necessary to operate the app:

  • Anthropic — processes video frames to generate mechanics analysis. Anthropic does not retain submitted frames for model training.
  • Render — hosts our backend servers in the United States.
  • Gmail — delivers analysis results if you provided an email address.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

4. Data Retention

  • Name and email: stored locally on your device only; deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Video frames: not stored on our servers after analysis is complete.
  • Analysis results: not stored on our servers; saved locally on your device only.
  • Error reports: retained for up to 12 months, then permanently deleted.

5. Children's Privacy

Softball Mechanics is designed for use by coaches, parents, and guardians who analyze players they work with. The app may be used to analyze youth softball players.

Email collection is entirely optional. If you choose to provide a child's email address or your own on behalf of a child under 13, you confirm that you have parental or guardian consent to do so. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without consent, please contact us at SoftballMSupport@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is protected using encrypted HTTPS connections. API keys and secrets are stored as environment variables and are never embedded in the app binary. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete local data: uninstall the app to remove all locally stored name, email, and analysis history.
  • Opt out of email delivery: simply leave the email field blank in the app settings.
  • Request error report deletion: email us at SoftballMSupport@gmail.com.

California residents (CCPA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of sale (we do not sell data).

EEA / UK residents (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

8. Third-Party Services

The app does not display advertisements or use tracking pixels. We have no affiliation with USA Softball, NPF, or any other softball organization.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users through the app.
